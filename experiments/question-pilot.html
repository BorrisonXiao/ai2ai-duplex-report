What this pilot will compare

The target is roughly 24 conversation points across the same four AMI meetings and two earnings calls. We will look near five, eight and ten minutes and at a later point, choosing completed speech and natural opportunities. A fixed time is a control, not a reason to invent a question that does not fit the discussion.

  1. Ordinary reminders: two short, useful questions using all earlier speech.
  2. Connected evidence: select two related facts, then write a question that needs their connection.
  3. Rewriting: start from an actual simple question and add a necessary connection, preserving a natural spoken request.

This offers up to 144 candidate wordings before source review. Alternative rewrites do not count as new information needs. The final number depends on source support and naturalness.

Reader checks will hide the proposed answer and selected evidence. Early questions may also be checked against longer transcript prefixes, provided later discussion has not changed the answer. Those are offline context controls, not claims that the same participant naturally asks the same question repeatedly.

Selected AMI questions will use past speech from the actual requester to condition F5-TTS. Original context and the continuation will be copied around the inserted question. The original meeting pauses while the question plays; answer audio remains deferred.
