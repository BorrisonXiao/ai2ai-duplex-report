Timing and counting rules

Each question counts once using its first retained representative in the existing report. Use the recorded planned replay time where it differs from the transcript cutoff; this affects two EN2003a outreach questions. Eight earnings-call positions move to a nearby estimated word boundary for audio insertion; their original transcript cutoffs are preserved. Other rows use the saved question cutoff. Alternative phrasings and later repeats do not reweight the statistics.

Same meeting and same facts needed for a complete answer count as one question. Similar topics, partial fact overlap and generic request templates do not by themselves imply the same question.

Two original outreach questions use the later planned replay point, 26:18.92, instead of their 24:49.99 transcript cutoff. AMI durations come from official meeting metadata; call fractions use the complete Earnings21 recording durations. The current exports add the spoken cue where missing and use the recorded audio pause position. Original question-generation texts and transcript cutoffs remain preserved.
