What the expansion shows

Retained 23 new question wordings over 15 information needs: 15 assistant-authored and eight local 30B wordings with source-reviewed references. These span four additional AMI meetings and two earnings calls. They are simulated requests, not original human dialogue.

Short questions can be useful without forcing a multi-part summary: “What was that tool for transforming the XML?” and “Did we ever settle on the company colour?” ask for facts that earlier discussion supplies. Requester motivation and the actual cutoff are checked separately from fluency.

The 30B reader is a useful diagnostic, but cannot certify its own labels: it still swaps a nearby 34% learning-difficulty statistic for the actual 50% remote-loss figure and fails earlier capacity/ownership questions. Thirty-three question-only calls cite nonexistent context. Manual source review remains necessary.

Two real document lookups run through a local search/open backend. They establish source pages and availability gates, not an autonomous model tool policy. No new audio or answer TTS is generated.

From a conversation prefix to a checked question

The reader is Qwen3-30B-A3B-Instruct-2507, a non-thinking 30B mixture-of-experts checkpoint. vLLM runs BF16 weights with prefix caching and CUDA graphs, a 49,152-token context and an 8 GiB KV cache. Reader output is capped at 1,536 tokens. The maximum audited reader prompt is 30452 tokens.

The local generator and reader use the same checkpoint. Reading a local draft is therefore a consistency check, not independent model judging. Primary-assistant review is also not a human naturalness rating. The new meetings are development data; prompt/source selection was revised after inspecting drafts, so there is no blind generalisation claim.

A concrete route to document tools

ConEC is the closest immediate match because it pairs the calls with real slides and releases. A local read-only backend implements search_documents(call, time, query) and get_page(call, time, document, page). It returns page text, a source URL and the PDF hash. Earlier speech must announce the document as available on the company website; this is availability evidence, not an archived upload timestamp.
