What changed since the original proposal

Review cutoff: 30 September 2026. Coverage prioritizes full-duplex architectural diversity and reproducible artifacts from 2024–2026, with older conversation corpora where they remain useful. Only the Literature review collection is present; project experiments and a new implementation proposal are outside this page.

The supplied March 16 proposal connects a streaming encoder to a reasoning module and studies semantic sufficiency, hypothesis revision, and speaker/stream-aware policies. Its central outcome is a correct first useful response with appropriate conversational timing. This review refreshes the cited work and identifies existing components that could support those questions.

The literature supports several concrete paths beyond the original Moshi-based study. For a text reasoning backbone, MiniCPM-o 4.5 exposes a streaming encoder and lightweight speech decoder, while BayLing-Duplex expresses dialogue control in a standard autoregressive vocabulary. Lychee-FD retains native parallel modeling but separates deeper semantic, acoustic and control parameters. Realtime-Venus and DuplexOmni make asynchronous backend reasoning an explicit system component. [22] [24] [26] [27] [23]

Moshi already has an LLM backbone. The architectural comparison is therefore about how time, concurrent streams and speech generation are represented, rather than whether a model uses an LLM. In particular, a text-only output vocabulary can coexist with continuous audio input and a separate speech decoder; “codec-free” usually describes the LLM interface, not necessarily the complete synthesizer. [2] [22] [8]

The March proposal already cites Think while listening, now an ICLR 2026 paper. It directly studies early CoT and semantic sufficiency, while its DPO stage also addresses adaptive reasoning. Think-Aloud adds playback-driven progress speech during reasoning, but does not implement interruption handling. The strongest remaining question in the reviewed evidence is how a model revises a hypothesis when a delayed constraint arrives from the relevant speaker amid overlap, or while a delegated job is still running. This is a synthesis of the reviewed sources, not a claim of exhaustive novelty. [14] [33] [27]

Evaluation now extends well beyond single-turn spoken reasoning: τ-Voice measures grounded customer-service outcomes, FDB v2/v3 test corrections and tool use, and September’s Duplex-MPE directly targets multi-party addressing and silence. These suites complement VoiceBench and MMAU-Pro; a high score on one does not imply strong performance on another. [18] [15] [20] [33] [3] [12]

A finer distinction than “Moshi versus LLM decoder”

Moshi’s temporal model is initialized from the Helium text LLM. The more useful comparison is parallel streams versus serialized tokens, text versus speech prediction in the main backbone, and internal versus delegated reasoning. An “inner monologue” aligned to spoken words is also not automatically a private chain of thought. [2] [14] [22] [24]

Full duplex during speech is not full duplex during tools

VoiceChat’s paper/card restrict barge-in during tool execution. The NVIDIA frontend–backend design suppresses foreground output during backend execution. Realtime-Venus explicitly targets continued interaction under asynchronous delegation. Treat these as different experimental conditions when studying late corrections. [30] [29] [27]

Review synthesis

Speaker-conditioned revision under late evidence: determine whose correction should update which hypothesis while preserving timing.

Think while listening addresses early CoT/DPO; FDB v1.5 and Duplex-MPE address overlap/addressing. These reviewed sources do not jointly establish causal speaker-specific reasoning revisions. [14] [11] [33]

Review synthesis

Measure useful answer timing alongside correctness and commitment errors rather than rewarding a fast filler or an early wrong answer.

SRQA measures reasoning delay; FDB v3 separates first response, tool and completion latency; τ-Voice measures task success and voice interaction. Their metrics are complementary. [14] [20] [18]

Review synthesis

Jointly validate revised task state, returned backend results and queued speech after a late correction.

DuplexOmni already stops/resets background thinking; Realtime-Venus and Context Spanning provide delegation/injection mechanisms. VoiceChat prevents barge-in during tool execution. Cancellation alone is not the missing contribution: test listening freshness and output admission together. [23] [27] [34] [30]

Review synthesis

Construct natural-acoustic prefix supervision with stable session/speaker splits and auditable sufficiency/revision labels.

SmoothConv/DuplexConv and otoSpeech preserve channels or timing; SRQA/CoT construction uses synthetic speech. Existing corpus labels are not automatically reasoning-sufficiency ground truth. [14]

These are gaps relative to the reviewed evidence, not validated claims that nobody has studied them. A full novelty audit would be needed before selecting a new project contribution.
