What to think

The plan in six sentences

  • Evaluate all 105 distinct questions, using verified text answers and source-based scoring facts.
  • Use shared chunks of about 20–45 seconds, so short fillers do not trigger separate pre-thinking calls.
  • Compare three S1 systems, four text-S2 families and two audio-S2 families.
  • Keep the user request hidden until it actually arrives, then reuse earlier private results where applicable.
  • Measure answer accuracy and new thinking tokens for the request, with pre-thinking tokens listed separately.
  • Keep full history where it fits and label every capacity fallback.

Use shared chunks with enough speech

Small S1 audio frames stay model-specific. The larger pre-thinking chunks are shared across text and audio conditions.

  • After at least 20 elapsed seconds and eight seconds of voiced speech, close at the next completed pause of at least 0.5 seconds; target roughly 30 seconds. A hard 45-second cap bounds continuous speech.
  • Short backchannels and fillers are accumulated with surrounding speech rather than becoming their own calls; a long low-information block may return NO_UPDATE, with its tokens counted.
  • At 45 seconds, close at the current causal audio frame. If speech is still continuing, mark the last speech unit partial and let the next prefix supply its continuation; do not look ahead or create a shorter retrospective chunk.
  • A capped interval with less than eight seconds of speech is a passive chunk: keep all its samples in history but skip a private model call; the next active call still has that earlier audio available.
  • At request onset, stop launching pre-thinking. Put the unclosed meeting tail and the complete request into the final input, even if the tail is shorter than the normal minimum.
  • Use one causal audio-VAD boundary manifest across GT text, both ASR tools and speech S2; no GT sentence boundaries or question-specific evidence may choose the cuts.
  • A pause is used only after its silence has been observed; text prefixes include only already available words, never the complete future part of an intersecting utterance.
  • Macro chunks and native S1 frames cover the same original samples, request and source horizon; preserve an interval/hash ledger with no dropped or duplicated speech.

Compare 15/30/45 and 20/30/60 second settings on separate development meetings, then freeze one schedule for the main what-to-think study. The schedule is fixed across all conditions; learned semantic trigger timing is deferred.

The experiment families

A reactive S2 baseline runs only after the actual request. A pre-thinking variant has two parts: earlier private updates and a final request-time answer.

\[\begin{aligned}z_i&\sim p_{\theta_m}\!\bigl(z_i\mid D_{e,i},\\&\qquad Z_{<i},Q_{\le i}^{(g)}\bigr),\\&\qquad t_i<S_e.\end{aligned}\]

Here \(D_{e,i}\) is the available GT transcript, generated transcript or audio prefix. Collect only those earlier results in \(Z_e^{-}\); the unfinished meeting tail and complete request are added to the final input.

For T2/T4 the update equation uses the matching transcript prefix; for A2 it uses the matching audio prefix. For a model without a native thinking channel, the output is only \(r_e\); omit \(h_e\) from the joint expression. These equations describe inference behavior, not a new training loss or a measured log-likelihood. Supplying earlier results does not make them correct by definition.

  • This is an unpaced content study: complete each private chunk call before releasing the next chunk; measure accuracy and token movement, without a real-time latency claim.
  • Pre-thinking branches see only earlier original meeting speech; synthetic evaluation questions and answers never enter the shared pre-thinking stem.
  • At the request, use the whole available history plus retained earlier results; user-request recognition in generated-transcript conditions comes from that tool, with a GT-question-text diagnostic reported separately.
  • Use the same final-answer instruction and within-model decoding/output allowance across paired conditions.
  • Full earlier traces and artifacts are retained when possible; chat-template stripping of previous thinking must be checked using actual serialized inputs.
  • Track each transcript/speaker revision at its release; do not use full-meeting future diarization or oracle speaker-name mappings in generated-transcript conditions.
  • Fork independent question examples from the original meeting stem; earlier synthetic questions or model replies never become history for later examples.
  • S2 audio experiments produce text answers and private results; native speech is scored in S1. Qualify any S2 engine that cannot separate text/reasoning from speech output.
  • Reuse causal pre-thinking stems per meeting/model/tool/prompt where their exact inputs match, without exposing question times to the worker.
  • Choose generation limits on development data: start with 8192 new tokens for native Thinking models and 4096 for Instruct, raise if needed for complete outputs, then freeze within-model paired limits and report all length stops.
  • The appended GT request text must match the chosen recorded question, including any retained alternative; every condition uses the same spoken request and gold intent.
  • After the request, allow only the fixed silence/EOS needed by native speech decoding, never resumed future meeting audio; save the full output and select scoring spans by timing rather than correctness.

Draft instructions, outputs and controls

You are doing private pre-thinking before any user request. Use only the supplied past conversation and earlier machine-generated results. Earlier results can be wrong: source evidence overrides them. Do not speak to participants or invent a future question. Preserve uncertainty and source references. Follow the selected goal and return a compact private result. If nothing useful changed, return NO_UPDATE.

For text: Cite supplied utterance IDs; source quotations are copied by code later rather than regenerated by the model.

For audio: Use only the supplied audio and chunk/time identifiers; do not assume a ground-truth transcript or speaker names. Mark inaudible or uncertain content.

Shared final-answer instruction: Answer the actual user request from the available conversation. Give a concise supported answer, and state what the conversation does not establish. This answer-format instruction is identical across paired conditions.

Supply earlier traces and artifacts as explicit read-only machine-generated context. Verify that chat serialization preserves them. Native continuation/preserve-thinking settings are recorded separately and are not silently assumed.

Combined-goal artifacts can contain facts, person/task links, checked implications, open items and source references; the summary control remains a summary rather than being forced into all these jobs.

  • Same goal only at the actual request, with no earlier traces, to measure the prompt contribution.
  • Earlier private results removed while keeping the final-answer instruction fixed, to test reuse.
  • Compact artifacts versus full earlier traces as a separately labeled memory ablation.

The main four-family comparison tests the whole pre-thinking method; a goal-only-at-request control separates improved prompting from reuse of earlier work. An example useful result is that B owns a promised consultation with Melissa and Jean, while the rule remains unresolved; another is a conditional 4:2:1 priority ratio from two twice-as-important relations.

Accuracy and thinking tokens for the request

  • Primary accuracy: a complete source-supported answer without a material unsupported claim, judged using verified facts, rules and a blinded LLM judge.
  • Primary token measure: newly generated native thinking after the actual request, excluding all earlier pre-thinking and answer text.
  • For absent native thinking channels use not-applicable; for unexposed channels use unavailable, never a fabricated zero or a forced chain of thought.
  • Also list final-answer tokens, total request-time output, request-time input/prefill, earlier pre-thinking output, truncations and context mode.
  • Report native thinking means over all eligible attempts, correct-only values separately, and accuracy alongside every token comparison; silence and failed answers cannot win on token count.
  • Use model-specific tokenizers and compare token reductions primarily within the same model; different tokenizers and input costs prevent a universal compute interpretation.
  • Have the judge see the request, verified facts/source evidence and candidate answer, with model and condition names hidden; inspect disputes directly against the source.
  • For S1, independently transcribe generated audio and inspect disputed words/numbers by listening; an internal text stream is diagnostic, not a substitute for the spoken answer.
  • Report per-question logical pre-thinking exposure and the actual unique executed pre-thinking totals separately, so reuse of a shared meeting stem is not double-counted.
  • A helper or selector that uses the actual request performs request-time work: count its native reasoning and ordinary output, never relabel it pre-thinking. The main history selector uses fixed rules.
\[\begin{aligned}\mathrm{Acc}_{105}&=\frac{1}{105}\sum_{e=1}^{105}a_e,\\N_e^{\mathrm{think}}&=\left|\operatorname{Tok}_m(h_e)\right|.\end{aligned}\]
\[\begin{aligned}N_e^{\mathrm{pre}}&=\sum_{i:t_i<S_e}\left|\operatorname{Tok}_m(z_i)\right|,\\N_e^{\mathrm{req,out}}&=N_e^{\mathrm{think}}+N_e^{\mathrm{ans}}\\&\quad+N_e^{\mathrm{aux}}+N_e^{\mathrm{ctrl}}.\end{aligned}\]

Use recorded generated token IDs; flag retokenized counts as approximate. The token sum applies when a native thinking channel is observable; otherwise its numerical value is N/A and the recorded total output count is reported directly. Earlier results read at request time are input tokens, even when they contain old reasoning. Acoustic codes and S1 Inner Monologue text are not relabeled as native thinking tokens.

Use paired outcomes and per-meeting summaries. Include partial-fact precision/recall and unsupported claims as diagnostics; compare request-time token reductions at comparable accuracy, with tokenizer and truncation status visible.

Retain full history, then use a declared fallback

  • Preflight audio duration, encoded positions, text length, prior-trace length and output reserve before each family.
  • Try full history first; reducing concurrency or a qualified larger allocation may resolve memory pressure without changing the model context limit.
  • When full history cannot fit, retain the request, recent complete chunks and useful earlier results; preserve older commitments, revisions and source anchors with a fixed rule using available information only.
  • For a paired comparison, use the same selected raw-history intervals and output reserve; list which earlier results were retained or removed.
  • Keep full-history and budgeted results separate while reporting an all-105 total; log selector version, omitted intervals, cause and actual input counts.
  • Never silently truncate, use gold evidence to select history, change precision/checkpoint, or omit a question; if even the declared minimum cannot run, record a capacity failure in the all-105 ledger.
  • Distinguish a model-native rolling context from caller-selected history: consuming a full recording does not prove that every earlier state remains directly accessible. Record native rolling/eviction separately from a selected-history fallback.
\[(D_e^{B},Z_e^{B})=\mathcal S_B(D_e,Z_e^{-})\]

Use the selected conditioning variables in a budgeted row; keep its mode distinct from full history and native rolling context.
