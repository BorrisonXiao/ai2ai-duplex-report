# Question expansion sources

Synthetic questions and proposed references were generated and reviewed for this project. They are not original participant requests or human-annotated ground-truth answers.

AMI source: [AMI Meeting Corpus](https://groups.inf.ed.ac.uk/ami/corpus/), Jean Carletta and AMI project contributors; [official CC BY 4.0 terms](https://groups.inf.ed.ac.uk/ami/corpus/license.shtml). This study uses manual word/speaker annotations from EN2002b, ES2002b, IS1000b and TS3003b, with brief supporting transcript excerpts and synthetic reference labels. No new AMI audio is hosted.

Earnings source: [ConEC](https://github.com/huangruizhe/ConEC), revision 88440713d8b80dc4f19b225f6480237e78c379de, based on [Rev Earnings-21](https://github.com/revdotcom/speech-datasets/tree/main/earnings21). The original Rev license covers transcripts and associated alignment text under CC BY-SA 4.0. Public accessibility does not establish blanket rights to third-party call audio or PDFs. Raw call transcripts, full documents, participant rosters and audio are not redistributed in this bundle. Questions, concise factual paraphrases, source IDs and source links support the review.

Local model: [Qwen3-30B-A3B-Instruct-2507](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3-30B-A3B-Instruct-2507), revision 0d7cf23991f47feeb3a57ecb4c9cee8ea4a17bfe, Apache 2.0. The checkpoint and vLLM inference are local; this page makes no model calls.
